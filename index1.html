About the Program

Transforming lives, one child at a time.

Education, a fundamental right, often faces challenges like inadequate infrastructure, limited access, and financial constraints.

India's alarming dropout rate — 12.6% of students leave school prematurely, per the Indian National Statistical Office.

Children from families affected by sudden tragedies — the loss of a breadwinner or a major health crisis — are particularly vulnerable.
