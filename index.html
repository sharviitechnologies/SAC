our heart · since 2004

Where classrooms preserve looms.

In Karnataka, daughters of handloom weavers face an impossible choice: leave school to learn the loom, or leave the loom to finish school. Our sponsorship pairs each child with a scholarship and an artisan grant — so the family's craft survives the same generation that breaks its poverty cycle.

We call it continuity education. 79% of our weaver-family graduates return home as teachers, designers, and cooperative leaders.
