About the Program

Every child deserves a chance to learn

Education is a fundamental right, yet millions of children in India face barriers that force them to drop out. Sudden family tragedies, loss of income, or a simple lack of resources can end a child's education overnight.

The Sponsor a Child (SAC) program, initiated by Sewa International, bridges this gap by providing direct financial support to students from grades 1 to 12. Since inception, we have positively impacted over 25,000 children across 12 Indian states.

Simple Process

Four steps to transform a life

From choosing a child to watching their dreams unfold — every step of your sponsorship journey is clear, direct, and deeply meaningful.

01

Choose a Child

Browse profiles of children from grades 1–12 who need support to continue their education.

Each profile includes the child's age, grade, state, specific need, and their dream for the future.

02

Make a Donation

Sponsor a child with a one-time or recurring contribution. Every rupee goes directly to education.

Sponsorships typically range from $25–$75/month. 100% of your contribution reaches the child.

03

Track Progress

Receive updates on your sponsored child's academic achievements and milestones throughout the year.

You'll get periodic reports, photos, and personal messages from the child and their family.

04

Transform a Life

Watch your investment grow into a brighter future — for the child, their family, and their community.

Alumni of the program go on to higher education and careers that change the trajectory of entire families.
